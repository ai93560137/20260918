                Overview
                Structured Products

                Structured products are a major part of HKEX’s growing product ecosystem. Explore their features and benefits and learn how HKEX structured products can meet your investing needs.

                Overview Trading Information and Historical Reports Education and Resources

                What's new?


                Learn more about the Silver Derivative Warrants (search: SILVER)

                Overview
                • Listed structured products are financial instruments issued by eligible issuers under the Listing Rules (Issuers) and traded on HKEX’s cash market. 
                • The payoff of structured products is based on the performance of the underlying asset(s), which can be stocks, stock indices, fixed income, currencies, commodities or a basket of assets. 
                • Structured products can be designed to allow investments under different expectations on price movements of underlying asset(s), such as direction and range. 
                • The maximum level of investors’ capital at risk will not exceed the initial investment amount.

                The following listed structured products are currently available for listing and trading on the equity market of HKEX:

                • Derivative Warrants (DWs) 
                • Callable Bull / Bear Contracts (CBBCs)
                • Inline Warrants (IWs)

                 


                Learn more
                Why invest in listed structured products?

                Capital efficiency

                The price of a structured product is only a fraction of the price of the underlying asset and trading structured products involves leverage, enabling investors to achieve varying levels of exposure to underlying assets at a relatively low cost. 

                In Hong Kong, trading structured products does not require margin, and stamp duty is exempted.

                 
                Investment diversification

                The underlying assets of structured products cover a variety of asset classes and regional exposures, allowing investors to access not only local assets in Hong Kong but also global opportunities.

                 
                Risk management

                With various asset exposures, leverage levels, and payoff structures, structured products can be used as a risk management tool to supplement investors' portfolios.

                 
                Variety of trading strategies
                Investors can adopt a variety of strategies when trading structured products. Investors should have a basic knowledge of those trading strategies, as well as risks involved in them.
